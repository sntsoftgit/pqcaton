## How to sign

Leave the following sentence as a comment on your pull request, exactly as written. A bot
records it.

```
I have read the CLA Document and I hereby sign the CLA
```

You sign once. Later pull requests do not need it again. The record is kept in
`signatures.json` in this repository.

## Contributing on behalf of a company

If you contribute as a team, or your employer wants a separate confirmation, a **corporate
agreement** is used instead of this individual one. Please write to **kty@sntsoft.co.kr**.
